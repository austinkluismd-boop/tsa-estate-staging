The philosophy

Balance over template. The goal is a nose that fits your face — proportion, projection and airflow considered together. In some cases surgery may also improve breathing; that is assessed honestly, never promised.

The aftercare

Splint about a week; most patients present publicly by 7–10 days. Swelling resolves in stages — the refined tip is a months-long reveal, and we tell you that upfront rather than promising overnight results.

Cost, answered

What rhinoplasty actually costs.

Nationally, ASPS members report an average rhinoplasty surgeon fee of $7,637 (2023 statistics), with the 2024 survey moving to a projected range of $7,500–12,500 — figures that exclude anesthesia and facility. We publish those so you can plan honestly; your own quote here is personal, all-in, and written at consultation.

Questions

Asked at nearly every consult.

Will it look “done”?
The goal is the opposite — a nose in proportion. The gallery above is the argument: drag the lines and judge whether the results announce themselves.
How long is recovery, really?
Splint for about a week. Most patients are publicly presentable at 7–10 days. Residual tip swelling refines over months — we show you the timeline at consult.
Can breathing be improved at the same time?
In some cases, functional issues can be addressed during the same operation. It is evaluated at examination — assessed honestly, never promised.
Who actually does my surgery?
Your operating surgeon is named at consultation and on your paperwork — and the gallery attributes every published case to its surgeon, so you can see each surgeon’s own work.
