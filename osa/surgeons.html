The surgeons · Oklahoma City

Seven surgeons. One standard.

Every Tulsa Surgical Arts surgeon sees Oklahoma City patients — led by the founder, Dr. Angelo Cuzalina, with Dr. Carisa Champion and Dr. Chase Nelson operating here through the week and Dr. Trent Fogleman full-time at this campus. Consults are virtual or in person; surgery happens in Oklahoma City or Tulsa — your choice, one standard. Credentials below are quoted from primary sources; where a detail isn’t yet verified, the card says so instead of guessing.
