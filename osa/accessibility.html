Accessibility statement.

This site is built to WCAG 2.1 AA and tested with automated and keyboard audits on every release.

What is built in

  • Full keyboard operation — including the before/after comparison sliders, video players, filters and menus, with a visible gold focus indicator.
  • Color contrast verified computationally for every text/background pair in both the dark and ivory registers.
  • A skip-to-content link on every page, labeled landmarks, and named controls for assistive technology.
  • Reduced-motion support: all animation is disabled when your system requests reduced motion, and all content is readable without JavaScript.
  • Every meaningful image carries descriptive alternative text; decorative flourishes are hidden from screen readers.

Known limitations

Third-party surfaces — the booking scheduler, consultation forms, the store, and social-media embeds — are operated by their vendors, and their accessibility is governed by those vendors. If any of them blocks you, call us and we will take care of it by phone.

Need assistance or found a barrier?

Call 405-751-0042 or call the campus line. Consultations and any information on this site are always available by phone, and printed material can be provided on request.
