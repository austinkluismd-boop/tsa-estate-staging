Website privacy policy.

This policy covers oklahomasurgicalarts.com — the Oklahoma City campus of Tulsa Surgical Arts. It describes what this website actually does — no more, no less.

What this website collects

The pages of this website are informational and do not, by themselves, set cookies, run advertising trackers, or collect personal information as you read. When you tap a phone, text, or email link, the conversation happens on your own device and carrier — not through this website.

Forms and booking

Consultation requests and bookings run on established third-party services the practice uses: NextPatient (appointment scheduling), JotForm (consultation request forms), and the practice’s online store. Information you submit there is governed by those services’ own privacy policies and by the practice’s patient-privacy obligations. Please do not include detailed medical history in a first request form — the consult team will collect what is needed through proper channels.

Embedded media

Video players load from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) domain only after you press play. Social media embeds (TikTok, Instagram) load their platform scripts only when the page is served online; those platforms may set their own cookies under their own policies when their embeds load.

Analytics

If the practice enables website analytics, it will measure page-level usage (such as which pages are visited and which buttons are tapped) to improve the site. Analytics are not used to make medical decisions and are never a substitute for the practice’s patient-privacy obligations.

Your health information

This website is not a patient portal and is not the place to send medical records. The practice’s Notice of Privacy Practices — the document that governs protected health information — is available from the practice directly.

Patient photography

Every before-and-after image on this website is published with written patient consent, and clinical photographs are never altered by artificial-intelligence tools.
