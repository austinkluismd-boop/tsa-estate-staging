The programs

Four doors into wellness.

GLP-1 Weight Management

GLP-1 therapy for sustainable weight loss — the practice’s wellness page cites benefits of “sustainable weight loss,” “improved blood sugar regulation,” and “reduced risk of cardiovascular disease,” all managed with labs and follow-up.

  • Eligibility, verbatim: “a BMI of 27 or higher with weight-related health concerns, or a BMI over 30”
  • Plus commitment to lifestyle change — candidacy is a clinical decision
  • Harmony™ — appetite control & sustainable weight loss
  • Revolution™ — next-generation metabolic peptide support

Peptide Therapy

Four physician-guided tracks: Recovery & Repair · Skin, Hair & Anti-Aging · Immune & Longevity Support · Growth-Hormone Optimization.

  • Begins with the $99 consultation and laboratory work
  • Dosing personalized and supervised — no protocol without evaluation
  • FDA status stated plainly on every education page — education is not a promise

Bio-identical HRT

Comprehensive hormone replacement — “capsules, pellets, topical creams, or injections” — including pellet therapy and nutraceuticals.

  • Symptom + lab-panel driven, retested on schedule
  • Delivered inside a practice with surgical-grade oversight

Laboratory & Follow-up

Baseline panels before therapy, scheduled re-testing during it — 10% off testing under the current special.

  • Your numbers decide the plan — and whether the plan continues
  • Records live in your chart at the practice, not an app
