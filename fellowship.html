The fellowship

Where America’s cosmetic surgeons are made.

Tulsa Surgical Arts runs one of only sixteen general cosmetic surgery fellowship programs listed nationally by the American Board of Cosmetic Surgery — accredited by the American Academy of Cosmetic Surgery, directed by Dr. Angelo Cuzalina with co-director Dr. James Koehler. Its graduates now run “Surgical Arts” practices across the country.

Fellows at a time2 — staggered Jul 1 / Jan 1
Case volume1,000+ procedures per fellow
Operating tempo2–3 majors/day · Mon–Thu
PrerequisiteAlready board-certified surgeons
Graduates becomeABCS board-eligible

“Training Fellows are taken on July 1 and January 1 so that one Training Fellow always has a 6-month seniority their last 6 months… The average Training Fellow is directly involved in over 1000 surgical procedures over the course of the program.” — AACS program page · “Angelo Cuzalina, MD, DDS / Co-Director: James Koehler, MD, DDS” — ABCS national fellowship register · “eligible to become board certified by the American Board of Cosmetic Surgery” — practice fellowship page

The lineage

A teaching tree you can audit.

Cosmetic surgery is taught hand-to-hand. This tree starts two generations up — and every card below quotes the alumnus’s own published bio, with the link to prove it.
